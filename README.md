# ticker-tape-web

Bloomberg Terminal-inspired financial data platform in the browser: streaming quotes, multi-asset charts, newswire, research, and portfolio analytics.

**[Live Demo →](https://jeffbai996.github.io/ticker-tape-web/)**

![ticker-tape dashboard](docs/images/hero.jpeg)

## Overview

`ticker-tape-web` brings institutional-style market monitoring, news triage, and portfolio analytics to desktop, mobile/iOS, and large-screen TV displays:

- **Command-Driven Terminal Footer (`ticker>`)** — jump directly to symbols (`NVDA`), technical overlays (`ta AMD`), intraday charts (`intra TSM`), relative performance comparisons (`vs AAPL MSFT`), price alerts (`alert SPY > 700`), and watchlists (`w SHOP`).
- **Real-Time Quotes & Multi-Timeframe Charting** — interactive candlestick and line charts, technical indicators, options chains, earnings calendars, and cross-asset market heatmaps.
- **Built-In Newswire & Research** — priority-ranked financial newswire, symbol-level catalysts, and AI-assisted research views.
- **Portfolio & Risk Analytics** — multi-currency book tracking, P&L attribution, concentration & exposure views, and interactive demo portfolios.
- **Cross-Platform Form Factors** — responsive desktop/tablet/mobile web app, native iOS wrapper, and 10-foot TV dashboard layout.
